# CloudZero — Brand & Content Brief

CloudZero is a boutique advisory and go-to-market (GTM) engineering firm.
We render services to the hyperscalers — AWS, Azure, and Google Cloud —
and to the silicon vendors — NVIDIA, AMD, Intel, and other accelerator
SKUs — to help them hyper-scale through their channel partner ecosystems.

## What we do
- Train AT the hyperscalers and silicon vendors: their field teams,
  their channel partners, and the engineers landing the technology.
- Match the right technology to the right use case across cloud and
  accelerated compute.
- Build the market, sell into it, and deploy when required.
- Operate as a senior, technically credible boutique — small team,
  serious work.

## How we work
- AI is a force multiplier across our research, enablement content,
  partner targeting, and field execution. Reference it as a capability
  we wield. Do not describe ourselves as bots or agents.
- Voice is human-led, AI-accelerated.
- Our engineering heritage is what makes our GTM advice credible. It
  shows up in the quality of the work, not in the marketing copy.

## Tone
Boutique, senior, confident, technical-but-readable. McKinsey meets
staff engineer. No buzzword soup. No "we are pioneers / disruptors /
revolutionizing." Plain, sharp sentences.

## Rewrite rules for the site
1. Treat this as a clean rebrand. Do not reference any past
   positioning, do not say "pivot," "transformation," "new chapter,"
   or anything that implies we used to do something else. Public
   audience meets us as we are today.
2. Replace any private-cloud / infrastructure-builder language with
   the GTM positioning above.
3. Propose 3–5 service offerings (e.g. Hyperscaler GTM Enablement,
   Channel Partner Activation, Technical Sales Enablement, Solution
   Architecture for AI/Accelerated Compute, Deploy Services). Write
   the page copy for each.
4. Keep existing CSS/layout intact unless content demands a change.
   Content first; redesign later.
5. On the Team page, wait for the explicit add/remove list before
   editing. Do not infer.
6. After edits, output a summary of what changed so it can be
   reviewed before pushing.
