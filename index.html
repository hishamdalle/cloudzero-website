What AWS assigns to a partner and what it means

When AWS invests in a partner, it assigns a team. Each member opens a different door. Most partners treat every AWS contact the same way — the fastest path to being quietly deprioritized.

Coverage, funding sponsorship, and a seat in strategic account conversations are earned through how you show up — not your tier. Knowing which door each person opens is where it starts.

PDM

Partner Development Manager

Your primary AWS relationship — and the one most partners underuse. Quietly gates tier, funding, and co-sell.

PSA

Partner Solutions Architect

Your technical sponsor in the room. The credibility that turns a maybe into a recommendation. Engage them before you need them.

PES

Partner Enablement Specialist

The fastest route through certifications and tier requirements — and it does not run through the AWS website.

Field

Designated field coverage

Named AWS coverage on your live deals. Earned through pipeline quality and presence — tier alone does not unlock it.

What AWS will not tell you

The things that matter and are never documented.

Patterns observed from inside the AWS partner development function, across hundreds of partner conversations, co-sell cycles, and program applications.

Co-sell · ACE

Your first engagement with an AWS account team is your most important one

AWS account teams form their view of you in the first interaction or two. A vague submission or an unprepared meeting costs credibility that takes far longer to rebuild than to earn. The opening matters more than partners think.

Resell · Channel

Resell without a demand plan adds friction, not revenue

Reselling without owning demand creates friction for AWS field teams and little payoff for you. There's a way to make resell pull revenue instead of adding overhead — and it turns on who owns the demand side.

Funding · Quota

Applying certain funding the wrong way can affect an account team's quota

Applied the wrong way, certain funding works against the very account team you need on your side — and no one explains why upfront. Get it wrong and sponsorship dries up, even on good deals. Get it right and it accelerates them.

2026 Programs

The New Customer Incentive replaced three programs on January 1 2026

AWS rewrote its incentive programs for 2026. Partners still operating on last year's logic are working from the wrong map — and leaving money on the table that's now there to claim.

MAP · June 2026

MAP now covers GenAI and agentic work and most partners missed the memo

MAP is no longer only for lift-and-shift migrations. Plenty of partners were told they weren't eligible and never went back. Many now are. That conversation is worth reopening.

ACE Eligibility

Without ACE Eligibility, AWS account teams cannot legally send you leads

There's a threshold that flips co-sell from one-way to two-way — the point where AWS starts bringing you opportunities instead of the reverse. Most partners submit into the void for years without knowing it exists. We know exactly where it sits.
