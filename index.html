Methodology

CloudZero operates at the intersection of three disciplines. Each one reinforces the others. Together they produce GTM motions that are defensible, measurable, and fast.
Foundation

Working Backwards

Start from the customer question, not the product feature. Write the press release before the roadmap. Define differentiation from the buyer's perspective. If you cannot articulate why a customer would choose you in one paragraph, the GTM strategy has not started yet.

Every opportunity qualifies through Metrics, Economic Buyer, Decision Criteria, Decision Process, Paper Process, Identify Pain, Champion, and Competition. No exceptions. Pipeline without qualification rigor produces forecasts that lie.
