About

CloudZero is a boutique advisory firm operating at the intersection of cloud, silicon, and channel. We were built for the part of the market where the technology is real, the buyers are sophisticated, and the difference between a deck and a deal is the quality of execution in the field.

We use AI heavily across research, enablement, partner targeting, and field execution. The leverage shows up in the speed and depth of our work; the judgment, the relationships, and the accountability remain human.

CloudZero is a minority-owned and woman-led firm.

Founder

Hisham Dalle | MBA, B.Eng

Founder

Hisham is a technology executive with over 20 years selling, architecting, and evangelizing enterprise compute across North America and the Middle East. He currently serves as Senior Azure Specialist at Microsoft Canada — owning the largest Azure territory in the country and winning Microsoft's AI Apps Visionary award — and was previously a two-time Sales Champion at both Cisco Systems and Nokia, with quota-busting streaks at each. Earlier, as Enterprise Architecture and Governance Leader at RSA Canada, he chaired the Architecture Center of Excellence through the company's cloud transformation.

Hisham founded CloudZero in 2010 and has grown it through strategic alliances with Accenture, Deloitte, and KPMG. The firm's flagship engagements include the decentralized national digital identity system for the Smart Dubai Government and the next-generation distributed-ledger services layer for the Government of Abu Dhabi. He is a McGill engineer and an AUB MBA, started his career at the Canadian Space Agency and Globalstar, and is trilingual across English, French, and Arabic.
