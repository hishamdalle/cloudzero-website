What AWS assigns to a partner and what it means

When AWS invests in a partner it assigns a team. Knowing who does what determines how you show up. Most partners treat every AWS contact the same way. That is the fastest path to being deprioritized.

AWS invests field time and co-sell energy in partners who arrive prepared, bring qualified pipeline, and hold a consistent point of view on a specific customer problem. Designated coverage, field sponsorship for funding, and inclusion in strategic account conversations are all earned through behavior, not tier.

PDM

Partner Development Manager

Your primary AWS relationship. Owns tier progression, funding access, co-sell motion, and field introductions. The relationship that unlocks everything else, and the one most partners underuse.

PSA

Partner Solutions Architect

Your technical counterpart. Validates solution design, supports Well-Architected reviews, and gives you credibility with AWS account SAs in live co-sell situations. Engage them before you need them.

PES

Partner Enablement Specialist

Drives your training, certification path, and accreditation completions. The fastest path through knowledge requirements for tier progression runs through this relationship, not through the AWS website.

Field

Designated field coverage

At Advanced and Premier tier, AWS assigns named account managers and Partner Solutions Architects to specific opportunities. This coverage is earned through consistent pipeline quality and field presence. Tier alone does not unlock it.

What AWS will not tell you

The things that matter and are never documented.

Patterns observed from inside the AWS partner development function, across hundreds of partner conversations, co-sell cycles, and program applications.

Co-sell · ACE

Your first engagement with an AWS account team is your most important one

AWS account teams form a view of a partner in the first one or two interactions. A vague ACE submission, an unprepared first meeting, or an unclear ask compounds quickly. Rebuilding credibility with a field team takes far longer than building it right the first time.

AWS co-sell program
Resell · Channel

Resell without a demand plan adds friction, not revenue

AWS field teams find resell motions operationally complex. They add billing layers, margin structures, and customer relationships to manage. A resell play without a clear customer segment and a demand generation plan creates friction for account teams without a payoff. Resell works when the partner owns the demand side.

2026 channel program updates
Funding · Quota

Applying certain funding the wrong way can affect an account team's quota

POC credits and certain MAP structures can count against an AWS account team's revenue targets if applied to workloads already committed. This is rarely explained upfront. Account teams become cautious about sponsoring funding requests from partners who do not understand the dynamics, even on legitimate opportunities.

AWS partner funding guide
2026 Programs

The New Customer Incentive replaced three programs on January 1 2026

AWS replaced the Partner Originated Discount, Public Sector Discount, and Customer Engagement Incentive: Grow with a single New Customer Incentive at the start of 2026. It also introduced a new Partner Growth Incentive for incremental resale portfolio growth. Partners running on 2024 program logic are working from the wrong map.

Full 2026 channel program breakdown
MAP · June 2026

MAP now covers GenAI and agentic work and most partners missed the memo

Since January 2026, MAP covers GenAI and agentic features built as part of modernizing existing applications, data, or workloads, not only cloud migrations. Partners told they were not MAP-eligible because they do not do lift-and-shift should revisit that conversation with their PDM using the current program scope.

MAP program details
ACE Eligibility

Without ACE Eligibility, AWS account teams cannot legally send you leads

Eligibility requires 10 validated partner-originated opportunities in a rolling 12-month window plus enrollment in an AWS Differentiation Program. Once achieved, the co-sell motion reverses direction: AWS starts bringing you opportunities. Most partners spend years submitting one-way into ACE without knowing this threshold exists.

New · February 2026

Concurrent Agreements Live

Buyers can now purchase the same product multiple times under separate agreements within a single AWS account. Different business units can procure independently with their own negotiated terms. Sellers can close multi-BU deals that previously stalled, transact mid-term expansions without waiting for renewal cycles, and eliminate workaround overhead. Professional Services: enabled by default. SaaS: integration update required.

New · April 2026

Partner Revenue Measurement via Marketplace Metering Live

AWS Marketplace Metering now automatically measures service consumption for AMI and ML products and feeds it directly into Partner Revenue Measurement. Partners get visibility into their AWS revenue impact and product consumption patterns without building separate tracking infrastructure or reconciling data manually.

SIs · Consulting partners

Channel Partner Private Offers (CPPO)

Consulting partners create and transact Marketplace private offers on behalf of ISVs at their own pricing and margin. The ISV sets the wholesale price; the partner controls the customer relationship and markup. No separate billing infrastructure required. 3% listing fee standard, 3.5% for CPPO transactions.
