Revenue Readiness

Three questions about your commercial model.

These determine whether your revenue compounds, or whether every quarter starts from scratch.

How many distinct revenue streams does your practice generate today?

+

Resell, professional services, managed services, IP licensing, Marketplace transactions, training. If you're running on a single stream, your growth has a ceiling built into it regardless of how good your delivery is. Multiple streams create resilience, compound independently, and give you leverage when one market shifts. The partners who scale past $5M in influenced ARR almost always have three or more streams working simultaneously.

Can you describe the business outcome you deliver without mentioning a technology?

+

The partners who grow fastest articulate the problem they solve and the outcome they deliver without defaulting to a feature list or a service catalog. The technology enables the outcome but it doesn't replace it. When a seller describes you to a customer, they won't list your certifications — they'll describe the result you produce. If that result isn't clear in one sentence, your positioning needs work.

After you close a deal, what's the defined motion that generates the next one from the same account?

+

A single transaction generates revenue once. A land-and-expand motion generates revenue continuously from the same customer without restarting the sales cycle. If you don't have this defined before you land the first workload, every deal starts from zero and your cost of acquisition never decreases. The flywheel only works when the expand motion is designed into the initial engagement.
